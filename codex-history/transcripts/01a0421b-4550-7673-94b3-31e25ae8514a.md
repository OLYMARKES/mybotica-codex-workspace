## Common questions

What information does a WHOIS lookup show?

Domain name, registrar, creation date, expiration date, last updated date, nameservers, DNSSEC status, and registrant details (when not privacy-protected). We also calculate domain age and days until expiry.

Why is registrant info hidden?

Many domain owners use WHOIS privacy protection (offered by registrars) to hide personal details. This is normal and legal. You'll see the privacy service's info instead.

What's the difference between WHOIS and RDAP?

RDAP is the modern replacement for WHOIS. It returns structured JSON data over HTTPS instead of raw text over TCP port 43. We use RDAP first (more reliable, better structured) and fall back to traditional WHOIS when needed.

How accurate is the expiry date?

Very accurate — we pull it directly from the registry. However, many domains auto-renew, so an approaching expiry doesn't necessarily mean the domain will become available.
