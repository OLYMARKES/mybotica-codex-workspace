## Design Specificity Verdict

The silhouette, dark-green and burgundy palette, long rectangular snout, wing, back spikes, and tail are strongly authored for MyBotica. The female distinction is restrained and preserves identity. The only unstable cue in V7 was the nearly horizontal direction of the two-lash fans.

## What's Working

- Strong, recognizable MyBotica silhouette even at reduced size.

## Priority Issues

- **P1 — Lash/whisker ambiguity:** V7 lashes left the eyes too horizontally. Fix: rotate each fan upward by 10–12 degrees while preserving common root and stroke weight.
- **P1 — Female cue at avatar size:** Full-body reduction makes lashes tiny. Fix: use a close head crop for the channel avatar while keeping the full-body master.
- **P2 — Raster softness:** Mild softness and baked cream background may be visible when composited. Fix later only if transparent production exports are required.
