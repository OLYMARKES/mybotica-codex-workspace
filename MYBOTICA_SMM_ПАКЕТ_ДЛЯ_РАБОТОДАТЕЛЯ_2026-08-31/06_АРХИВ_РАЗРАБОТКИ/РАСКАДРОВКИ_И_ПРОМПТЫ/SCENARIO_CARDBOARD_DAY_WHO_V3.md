# MyBotica Cardboard — «03:47», полная версия

Duration: 15 seconds. Format: vertical 9:16. Performance: stepped cardboard stop-motion, locked face, no facial animation.

1. **0:00–0:00,8** — Full-screen clock insert: `03:47`. Bright red digits. Night room tone.
2. **0:00,8–0:01,6** — Notification over the dragon at the laptop: `Клиент: «Добрый день!»`
3. **0:01,6–0:03** — Cut to the dragon’s face. It does not change or move.
4. **0:03–0:03,4** — Hard flash insert: `03:47`.
5. **0:03,4–0:05** — The same face, closer. Hold too long.
6. **0:05–0:05,4** — Hard flash insert: `03:47`, even larger and brighter.
7. **0:05,4–0:07,5** — Extreme close-up. Absolute silence. No blink, no mouth, no reaction.
8. **0:07,5–0:09,5** — The exact same close-up continues. Tired, flat voice: `День у кого?`
9. **0:09,5–0:10,5** — A second notification: `Клиент: «Вы работаете?»`
10. **0:10,5–0:11,8** — The dragon begins tipping sideways as one stiff cardboard piece. Very slow paper creak.
11. **0:11,8–0:13,2** — It hangs at an impossible angle for an awkward beat, then continues down.
12. **0:13,2–0:15** — Soft cardboard slap. The dragon lies flat and stays there. A small exact MyBotica logo appears. Hold to black.

Audio: quiet night room tone; two dry notification pops; tiny digital glitch on each `03:47`; silence before the line; one flat voice line; long cardboard creak; soft paper slap. No music.

Editing rule: each return to `03:47` is shorter, brighter, and more intrusive. Each return to the face is closer and held longer. The face itself never changes. The final fall must be slow enough to feel unnecessary.
