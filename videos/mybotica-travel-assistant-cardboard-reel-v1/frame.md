# Visual direction

Concept angle: a badly organized handmade traveler is surrounded by warm cardboard chaos, while MyBotica arrives as the one calm, precise dark-green layer in the frame.

- The generated tactile cardboard footage remains the visual truth.
- Human/problem copy sits on crooked cream paper labels with dark-green ink.
- MyBotica answers use solid dark-green cards with cream text and the exact logo mark.
- Typography is locally embedded Golos; headlines are heavy and large enough for Instagram feed viewing.
- Final frame is a clean dark-green brand field with the supplied white MyBotica logo, large slogan and one small deadpan footnote.
- No black, no gradients over the footage, no generic glassmorphism, no red character details.
