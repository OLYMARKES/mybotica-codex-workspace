# MyBotica launch frame

The brand character is a white hand-drawn bot-dragon with dark-green outlines
and burgundy horns, spikes, and wings. The video may temporarily reduce it to
pixel blocks, but the final form must feel faithful to the supplied character
sheet. Use warm cream rather than pure white, strong dark-green structure, and
small burgundy hits. Avoid cyberpunk blue, generic SaaS gradients, glossy 3D,
and dense interface chrome.
