# Visual direction

Concept angle: a badly organized handmade traveler is surrounded by warm cardboard chaos, while MyBotica arrives as the one calm, precise dark-green layer in the frame.

- The tactile cardboard dragon remains the visual truth.
- MyBotica answers use solid dark-green cards with cream text and the exact logo mark.
- Typography is locally embedded Golos and large enough for Instagram viewing.
- No black, no generic gradients, no glassmorphism, no red character details.
