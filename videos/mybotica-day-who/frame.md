# MyBotica — 23:30

The world is a warm miniature cardboard office at night: visible paper fibres,
cut edges, brass split pins, dim amber practical light and a moonlit window.
The exact cream-and-dark-green MyBotica cardboard boy is the visual truth. His
head spikes, wing membrane and tail spikes are deep denim blue `#2F6794`; he
never has eyelashes. The clock
inserts are the only aggressively bright element: red digital numerals on a
deep green cardboard display.

Every client message looks like a physical cream paper bubble with a thick
dark-green edge. Typography is large and functional because the Reel is read
inside a feed. No glossy UI, neon cyberpunk, white square behind the character,
extra ornament, smooth character acting or expressive facial animation.

Concept angle: the clock behaves like an intrusive thought while the dragon's
face refuses to react; the final slow fall is the only real action.

Focal element: unchanged dragon face or `23:30`, never both at equal weight.
Edge anchors: messages enter from the upper safe area; logo sits small in the
upper-left of the final lying shot. Background: generated cardboard footage,
kept unobstructed except for exact story overlays.
